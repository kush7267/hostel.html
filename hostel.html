▶ Hostel Rules & Instructions

  1. Students must carry their university ID card.
  2. Students must follow hostel timings.
  3. Ragging is strictly prohibited.
  4. Students must follow the warden's instructions.

▶ Hostel Admission Process

  1. Fill out the hostel application form.
  2. Enter your personal and academic details.
  3. Select your preferred hostel.
  4. Submit the application.
  5. Wait for hostel allotment.
  6. Complete the fee payment after allotment.
